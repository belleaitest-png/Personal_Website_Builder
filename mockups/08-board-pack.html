Executive summaryIf you only read one slide

You need someone senior who owns AI. You probably do not need them five days a week.

  1. AI makes money in a few specific places in your business. Most of the rest is a distraction, and someone has to say which is which.
  2. Restructuring teaches you to find value fast. The same habit works on AI: follow the money, ignore the demo.
  3. A fractional leader gets you senior judgment without a full-time hire. Strategy, a first move, and someone who makes sure it ships.

So whatStart with the one or two places AI pays. Park the rest without guilt.
