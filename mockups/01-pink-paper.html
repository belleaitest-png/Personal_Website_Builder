Mid-market chiefs get a senior AI lead, minus the full-time hire

Annabelle Body sits between the boardroom and the build, and says plainly where AI makes money in your business and where it is a distraction.

Annabelle BodyFractional AI Strategist

Most mid-market companies now have an AI line in the board pack and nobody whose job it is to make it true. The usual fixes are a hire the business is not ready for, or a pilot nobody owns.

Body offers a third route. A scientist turned accountant turned restructuring operator, she worked at Deloitte and AlixPartners before an MBA at Harvard Business School.

Restructuring leaves a habit: find the value fast and ignore the rest. She applies it to AI, in terms a board can approve and a team can ship.
