A Little About Me

From auditing balance sheets to reimagining the food system

I started my career at Deloitte in London, spending four years in audit and analytics before moving to AlixPartners, where I worked on some of the UK's most high-profile restructuring cases — including The Telegraph's forced sale process and Cineworld's Chapter 11 bankruptcy.

Six years restructuring broken businesses taught me one thing: systems beat intentions every time. That insight led me to Harvard Business School, where I co-founded a nutrition-focused CPG business in Startup Bootcamp and fell deeper into the question that drives everything I do now: why is it so hard to eat well when the science is so clear?

Before HBS, I spent a summer at Fruitist in LA, leading their nutrient density programme and building market intelligence tools for a $400M+ fresh fruit operation. I'm a competitive Hyrox and CrossFit athlete, a sailor, and someone who's hiked the Patagonia W trail and travelled solo through South America.
