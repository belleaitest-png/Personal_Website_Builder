About

I treat my life like a startup.

I'm Belle Body — British, HBS MBA, researcher, and full-time obsessive about the things that matter. I started by asking why food was making us sick, ended up down every rabbit hole from regenerative agriculture to biophysics, and haven't stopped since.

Before HBS: Deloitte, solo travel across three continents, and enough conferences to fill a library. My HBS section asked me to present on the food system because I wouldn't stop talking about it. That's usually how I know I'm onto something.

Whatever role I'm in, I'm simultaneously building my own portfolio of IP — writing, researching, and shipping ideas. I'm looking to work alongside people who are genuinely trying to change something.
